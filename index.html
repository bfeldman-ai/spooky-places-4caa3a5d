1. Sonoma Developmental Center

15000 Arnold Dr, Glen Ellen (Eldridge), CA 95442 · Map · ~1h25

  • Opened in the 1890s as a state home for "feeble-minded children"; one of California's largest institutions for people with developmental disabilities. Closed 2018.
  • Dozens of empty brick ward buildings across a large campus, plus a patient cemetery (Eldridge Cemetery).
  • Redevelopment is frozen in environmental review after a lawsuit; campus is decaying. Temporary fencing around some buildings due to copper theft and vandalism.
  • State-owned (Dept. of General Services). 650 acres of surrounding open space going to State Parks.

2. Old Sonoma County Hospital ("Chanate")

3325 Chanate Rd, Santa Rosa, CA 95404 · Map · ~1h20

  • Chosen as the county hospital site in the 1800s (hospital building by 1892); the main hospital dates to the 1930s. Later Sutter Medical Center, which moved out ~2014.
  • 17 buildings on a hillside; as of 2019, all but three abandoned. The last tenant left in spring 2026, so it's now mostly empty.
  • Chanate Historic Cemetery is on the property.
  • Ransacked, and squatters have used it. Privately owned (Chanate Development Group); housing project in environmental review as of July 2026, so its days may be numbered.

Videos: Freaky Abandoned Hospital & Insane Asylum… Santa Rosa, CA (described as "the old community hospital in Santa Rosa"; likely Chanate but not confirmed)

Reading: Gaye LeBaron on the hospital's history (incl. 1937 photo) · City of Santa Rosa project page · Press Democrat, 2019

3. Mare Island Naval Hospital

Hospital area near Touro University, 1310 Club Dr, Vallejo, CA 94592 · Map · ~1h

  • Main building (H1) completed 1899; contagious and general wards (H70, H72) added 1926. Served through the 1918 flu pandemic and both World Wars.
  • 46 buildings in the hospital complex; per naval historian Tom Snyder, Touro University uses only two or three. The rest are largely idle (not independently confirmed).
  • On the West Coast's oldest Navy base, closed 1996, with many other abandoned shipyard buildings nearby.
  • Privately owned (Touro University). Historic buildings that can't be torn down.

Videos: Abandoned Navy Hospital Mare Island · ABANDONED – Mare Island Navy Shipyard · Mare Island Military Base Abandoned Buildings

Reading: Vallejo Sun: history of the hospital · HABS record: hospital wards (H72) · James Tarry: Mare Island Abandoned (photos)

4. Kaiser Richmond Field Hospital

1330 Cutting Blvd, Richmond, CA 94804 · Map · ~45m

  • WWII hospital for Kaiser shipyard workers building warships; a birthplace of Kaiser Permanente. Closed September 1995.
  • One large, uninhabited building. A shorter outing than the other three.
  • Privately owned; part of Rosie the Riveter/WWII Home Front National Historical Park (exterior viewing only for the public).

Videos: None found specific to this building.

Reading: NPS · Wikipedia · Full HABS photo set (pharmacy, hallways, ambulance entrance)
